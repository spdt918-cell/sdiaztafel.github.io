Service & Community Showing up, doing the work, leaving things better

Some of the most formative experiences I've had weren't in a classroom. Volunteering internationally — building structures on a beach in the BVI, teaching English to kids in the Dominican Republic — taught me how to work in conditions you can't fully plan for, communicate across language and culture, and stay useful when things get hard.

Repaired a beach sunshade structure with a small team — no contractor, just hands and problem-solving. Also assisted a local rescue operation with puppy deticking and care, which turned out to be one of the more memorable things I've done.

Helped build and run a community education center from the ground up — construction during the day, teaching beginner English and running activities with the kids afterward. It pushed my Spanish, my patience, and my ability to lead without a clear playbook.
